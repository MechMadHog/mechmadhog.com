  <h2>⭐ Favourite Films</h2>
    <p>
      My favourite films are not necessarily the ones I think are technically
      the greatest. They are the films whose characters, worlds and ideas contain
      little pieces of my own personality.
    </p>
    <p>
      Sometimes it is a character I relate to or a particular characteristic I
      recognise in myself. Sometimes it is how relatable or self-aware the story
      feels, or simply how cool the characters and the world around them are.
    </p>
    <p>
      They are funny, strange, exciting and occasionally sad—but sad in a way
      that is still enjoyable to return to.
    </p>
