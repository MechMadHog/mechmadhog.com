<h1>🐉 Dungeons & Dragons</h1>

<p>
Dungeons & Dragons is one of my favourite hobbies. Every second Wednesday,
I meet up with the fantastic group from
<a href="https://www.instagram.com/mullingardnd?igsh=YnJ6dTE5M2dubnB1"
target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Mullingar Dungeons & Dragons</a>
to spend an evening rolling dice, fighting monsters, and creating stories
that couldn't exist anywhere else.
</p>

<p>
Our games are hosted in
<a href="https://www.instagram.com/clarkesbarmullingar?igsh=MTNsM3dhaGYwcm15dQ=="
target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Clarke's Bar, Mullingar</a>,
where we meet once a fortnight on Wednesday evenings.
It's a brilliant atmosphere with multiple tables running different campaigns,
making it easy for both new and experienced players to get involved.
</p>

<h2>⚔️ My Characters</h2>

<p>
I tend to gravitate towards martial characters, particularly Barbarians.
I enjoy being on the front line, soaking up damage and charging head-first
into whatever danger the Dungeon Master has prepared.
</p>
